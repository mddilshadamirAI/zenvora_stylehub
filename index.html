Our Story

About Zenvora Stores & Tabish Raja

Founder / Owner — Zenvora Stores

Zenvora Stores is an emerging online e-commerce retail brand operated by Tabish Raja, focused on bringing attractive, stylish, and affordable fashion products to customers through major online marketplaces.

With an emphasis on curated women's fashion including earrings, traditional jhumkhas, and elegant suit sets, Zenvora Stores is committed to trustworthy online retail and consistent customer satisfaction.

Customers can discover and purchase Zenvora Stores products seamlessly through leading digital shopping destinations including Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, as well as our official online brand store.
