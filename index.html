Our Story

A brand built around style, identity and expression.

ZENVORA STORES • Fashion & Jewellery

ZENVORA STORES is a fashion and jewellery brand focused on presenting beautiful styles through a distinctive, modern visual identity.

Our collections bring together jewellery, traditional-inspired pieces and Indian fashion, creating a catalogue where heritage and contemporary aesthetics can exist together.

Led by Tabish Raja, ZENVORA continues to build its identity around elegant presentation, expressive styling and a customer-focused approach across its marketplace presence.

Jewellery Earrings, jhumkhas and statement accessories.
Fashion Indian and contemporary ethnic-inspired styles.
Presentation Premium visual identity across collections.
Growth Building a recognizable fashion brand.
